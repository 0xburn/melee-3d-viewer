M'Overlay's default GameCube controller skin: its PNGs as it ships,
traced to vector outlines (paths.ts). The PNGs themselves are not in this
repository; paths.ts is all the viewer uses.

Modified (2026-10-02): paths.ts is each PNG traced to its own outline by
a tracing script - the edge where the texture's opacity crosses
half, interpolated between its pixels, kept to within 0.08 of a pixel of
it. Nothing is redrawn or recolored: the shapes are the textures' own, filled
in the colors default.lua tints them. The textures are 128 pixels; the
players draw them at about a fifth of that in a normal player, an eighth in
a small one, and past full size on a large screen, so drawn as pictures
they resample and their thin lines step and blur. Their outlines draw sharp
at every size.

The replay player draws them the way default.lua does
(src/components/viewer/Controllers.tsx): the outline of each
button and its pressed picture while held, in the skin's own colors, the
sticks moved over their gates, L and R filled by how far they are pressed.
